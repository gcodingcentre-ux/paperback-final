    Publishing with us

    Three ways to publish. Not sure which fits your book? Talk to us first, it is free.

    You lead

    Self-publishing

    You keep full control and full rights. We provide the services you choose: editing, cover and layout, ISBN, printing and online listing.

    • Pick only the services you need
    • You own your rights and set your price
    • Print runs from 100 copies
    Get started
    We share the work

    Hybrid publishing

    A partnership: you contribute to the production cost, and we bring our editors, designers and distribution network to the book.

    • Professional editing and design included
    • Bookshop and library distribution
    • Higher royalty share than traditional deals
    Get started
    Get it read

    Marketing

    A book needs readers. We plan the launch, set up your author page and online listings, and help you reach reviewers, bookshops and local media.

    • Launch plan and author profile
    • Social media and press outreach
    • Book launch and event support
