Refund policy

Last updated:

We want you to be happy with the work we do on your book. This policy explains when a refund is possible.

Before work starts

If you cancel within 7 days of paying and we have not started work, you receive a full refund.

After work starts

Once editing, design or layout has begun, we refund the part of the fee for work not yet done. Completed work is not refundable.

Printing

Printed copies cannot be refunded once printing has started, except where copies are damaged or not as per the approved proof. Report this within 7 days of delivery with photos, and we will reprint or refund.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method within 7 to 10 working days.
