Privacy policy

Last updated:

This policy explains what personal information PaperBack Publishers collects and how we use it.

What we collect

When you contact us or submit a manuscript form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, genre and the details you share about your book.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to you, discuss your book, provide publishing services and send updates you ask for. We do not sell your information.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us deliver your book (such as printers and payment processors), or when the law requires it.

Your manuscript

Your manuscript stays yours. We keep it confidential and use it only to evaluate and publish your work.

Your choices

You may ask us to view, correct or delete your information at any time by contacting us.
